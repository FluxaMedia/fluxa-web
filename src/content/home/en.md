:::taskcards
- [Install your first addon](/docs/addons-catalogs#installing-an-addon). Paste a manifest URL and make content rows appear.
- [Set up Nuvio sync](/docs/sync-accounts#nuvio-cross-device-sync). Keep library, profiles, collections, and progress aligned across devices.
