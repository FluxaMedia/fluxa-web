What it does

Everything in one place.

Fluxa brings together streaming, tracking, and library management without locking you into any service.

Community Addons

Install any Stremio-compatible addon to bring in movies, shows, and anime from sources across the web.

Watch Tracking

Auto-sync to Trakt, MyAnimeList, and Simkl. Every episode you watch is logged without lifting a finger.

Multiple Profiles

Up to five separate profiles on one install, each with its own library, addons, and accounts.

Built-in Player

Plays direct streams and torrents with mpv under the hood. Virtually every format, no plugins needed.

Smart Library

Save titles, build custom collections from addon catalogs, and continue exactly where you left off.

Skip Intro & Recap

Detects intro and recap segments automatically. One tap skips them — or ignore it and it disappears.

Desktop & Android

The same app, the same addons, on Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android. Pick up on any device.

Open Source

Free to use, free to inspect, free forever. Built on Rust and React, powered by the community.
