Glossary

Short definitions for common Fluxa, addon, playback, and source terms.

Addon terms

TermMeaning
AddonRemote service that provides catalogs, metadata, streams, or subtitles.
ManifestJSON document that describes an addon and its capabilities.
CatalogA row or browsable list of titles.
MetadataDetails like poster, synopsis, cast, trailers, episodes, and artwork.
StreamA playable source result returned after pressing Play.

Source terms

TermMeaning
DebridProvider that converts supported links or torrents into fast direct streams.
UsenetProvider/indexer ecosystem that can supply fast media sources.
CachedProvider already has the file ready to stream.
RemuxHigh-quality source with little or no extra compression.
WEB-DLVideo sourced directly from a streaming service.

App terms

TermMeaning
ProfileSeparate user space with its own library, addons, settings, and accounts.
LibrarySaved titles, continue watching, collections, and related history.
SyncOptional cross-device or third-party account update flow.
