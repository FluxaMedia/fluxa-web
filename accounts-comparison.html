Account Comparison

Which accounts are optional, what each one unlocks, and when to use them.

Quick comparison

AccountRequired?Use it for
No accountNoLocal profiles, local addons, local playback, local library.
NuvioNoCross-device Fluxa sync for profiles, library, progress, addons, and collections.
StremioNoUsing a Stremio account from the web account page when supported.
TraktNoMovies and TV watch history, lists, scrobbling.
MyAnimeListNoAnime lists and episode progress.
SimklNoMovies, TV, and anime in one tracking account.

Recommended setups

  • Single device: no account is required. Add tracking only if you want external history.
  • Desktop plus Android: use Nuvio if you want Fluxa state to follow you.
  • Anime-heavy use: connect MyAnimeList or Simkl.
  • TV and movie tracking: connect Trakt or Simkl.

Profile scope

Accounts are profile-scoped. One profile can connect a Trakt account while another profile uses a different account or no account at all. Switching profiles changes addons, library, settings, and connected services.
